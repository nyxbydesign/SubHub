Gag keyboard English and German frequency data

Adapted from Hermit Dave’s FrequencyWords (2018), commit
525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/tree/525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6/content/2018

The upstream lists are based on OpenSubtitles2018 from OPUS:
https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php

SubHub normalized case and Unicode, retained only bounded Latin single
words, combined English and German counts using their maximum, sorted
the combined table, and stored the data as a compressed TSV. The
reproducible converter is scripts/build_gag_wordlist.py.
